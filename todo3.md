## Interface
move "Retire this Runner" next to the name.
ask a control question if the player really wants to retire the player.
in mission briefings, raise the "Estimated payoff" next to the "Job" text, make it as big font as the "Job".
Change the pass to "Rest in Comfy Coffin Hotel"; charge 1 BOND; and roll for healing (7-9: heal if only 1 box; 10+: always heal; add combat modifier and any health gear or body modifications). You can do rest 4 times, but start a counter that tells that Person with most negative stat is starting to hunt you. The person has now Archenemy TAG If clock full fills, there is a hunt sequence (small mission) with following phases:
    * do you notice ambush? Social check, if failure combat starts (Skip avoid step), if 10+ success you can avoid with +2 ot combat +2.
    avoid (Stealth check to avoid combat)
    * direct combat, each time you succeed you make one wound to Archenemy. Combat is won when Archenemy takes 3 wounds. When archenemy has 2 wounds it tries to escape. If you want to follow, then driving test: 10+ you make the final 3rd wound and kill the Archenemy, if 7-9: you win the combat (=reset the clock) , but archenemy survives; 6 or below Archenemy escapes. 
        always possibility to choose RUN (Driving test) instead of comabt. If 10+ success, no damage and clock resets, if 7-9: 1 damage and clock resets; if below 6: 1 damage and loss of gear or 1-2 BOND.
    If you kill Archenemy, you get one Tier 2 (cost 2 BONDS/+2) weapon and get +3 boost and 2 BONDS.

## CREDS
change creds to BONDS. BOND is an abstraction of value. Simple items cost 1 BOND, and give +1; Better 2 BONDS, +2 to checks; State of art 3 BONDS, +3 to check.
Mission Payoff 1-3 BONDS (1 = easy; 2= medium; 3 = hard)
If you get 20 BONDS you will retire to a lunar colony. This is a game win, and should have appropriate text and flavors.
